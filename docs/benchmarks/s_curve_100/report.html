S-curve under a mid-path shove

Follow an S through four waypoints at the controllers' default speeds. Halfway through, another robot shoves ours sideways for 0.3 s. Each seed varies where the robot really starts (odometry believes it is exactly at the start), the battery charge, and the omni roller friction (field and wheel wear).

Distributions

Each curve shows the share of trials (y) that did at least as well as x: further left and higher is better. In the scatter plot, points far above the diagonal missed the target even though the robot knew where it was (a control problem); points near it missed because it didn't (a localization problem).
